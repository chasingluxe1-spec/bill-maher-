# Style — Investigative Crime Documentary (house style)

**Grounded in:** Quietly Deranged (NexLev RPM $9.71, the highest-RPM crime competitor —
visually decoded 2026-09-29) + the channel standing instructions. This is the "evidence-led,
victim-first" style, confirmed to match the winning competitor and the ethical brief.

## Structure (narrative spine)
Cold-open **verified contradiction/question** (facts, not gore) → **victim** (prompt, humane,
sourced background) → **the event** (what's established) → **the investigation** (what
investigators knew at each stage) → **the breakthrough** (the specific evidence that changed
the case) → **the outcome** (documented resolution, sentence, appeals) → **close** (answer the
opening question; name what remains uncertain). Never manufacture cliffhangers or withhold
essential context.

## Visual treatment (decoded from Quietly Deranged)
- **Material:** cinematic **stock footage** (mood/establishing) + real **static photos**
  (victim/people/places) + original **maps/timelines/evidence graphics**. No AI-as-evidence.
- **Pacing:** 5–8s per visual; slow, deliberate.
- **Camera:** Ken Burns slow zoom on nearly every shot; 2.5D parallax on key stills.
- **On-screen text:** **none** in the body (no caption bursts, no synthetic quote cards).
  Exception: map/timeline labels and clearly-marked document excerpts.
- **Transitions:** simple cuts + occasional cross-fades between related photos; short
  dip-to-black between acts.
- **Grade:** cool, desaturated; vignette + subtle grain on archival photos ("aged" true-crime).
- **Audio:** restrained music bed; narration intelligibility first; ≈ −14 LUFS; no spikes.

## Where the "punch" lives
All sensational energy is in the **title/thumbnail layer only** — never in the narration body.
The narration stays neutral, sourced, and humane. That split is why the format survives both
audience scrutiny and YouTube authenticity checks.
